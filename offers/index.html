Time-limited and expiring offers

These promotions, trials and expiring resources are not ongoing free tiers. They are excluded from the main directory counts. Check the provider for current eligibility, renewal fees and expiration rules. How we classify offers.

AF

AWS Free Plan

Cloud & VPS · Cloud
Time-limited offer
Free allowance

New eligible AWS customers: Free account plan for up to 6 months or until AWS Free Tier credits are exhausted; $100 signup credit plus up to $100 earned through activities

Keep in mind

The Free account plan is a time-limited ACCOUNT offer, not an always-free VPS. It ends at 6 months or credit exhaustion, whichever comes first; AWS automatically closes the Free account and resources/data become inaccessible unless upgraded. Joining AWS Organizations upgrades to Paid and ends Free Plan credit eligibility. Some services offer separate always-free monthly usage; Paid-plan usage beyond allowances can incur charges.

RF

Render Free Postgres

Databases · Cloud
Expiring free resource
Free allowance

One free Render Postgres database/workspace, capped at 1 GB; instance expires 30 days after creation

Keep in mind

Free Render Postgres is a TEMPORARY resource: it expires after 30 days, followed by a 14-day grace period before permanent deletion of the database and its data. There are no Free backups or managed connection pooling; not suitable for durable production data.

KW

Kinsta WordPress First Month

App hosting · Cloud
Time-limited offer
Free allowance

First month free for eligible new customers on select managed WordPress plans; Single 20GB includes 1 WordPress install, 20 GB server bandwidth/month, 10 GB storage and 125 GB CDN bandwidth/month.

Keep in mind

Not an ongoing free tier. Selected plans only; Single 20GB renews at $35/month on monthly billing after the first month, excluding taxes. Confirm eligibility, payment requirements and cancellation terms before signing up.

TM

tunnelto.me Free Hosted Tunnels

Networking · Cloud
Expiring free resource
Free allowance

The Free account is ongoing, but each hosted tunnel resource expires after one hour; unlimited one-hour tunnels on one custom domain, one tunnelto.me subdomain, 500 MB bandwidth/month, 100 requests/minute and 5 Mbps.

Keep in mind

Classified as an expiring hosted resource, not a persistent ongoing free tunnel: every Free tunnel terminates after one hour and must be recreated, so this belongs in Time-limited / expiring offers, not the primary ongoing directory. Persistent tunnels and built-in bearer/password authentication require paid Hobbyist; domain registration may cost extra. Pre-26-Aug-2026 accounts may retain 1 GB/month; commercial-use permission is not explicit.
