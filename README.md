## 🔎 What we'll compare

Each service entry should include:

- **Free plan:** Always-free tier, time-limited trial, or free credits.
- **Limits:** Bandwidth, storage, compute, requests, and any inactivity rules.
- **Credit card:** Whether payment details are required.
- **Custom domains:** Availability and HTTPS support.
- **Use cases:** Static sites, APIs, databases, containers, or other workloads.
- **Restrictions:** Commercial-use limits, region availability, and other important conditions.
- **Official links:** Pricing, documentation, and a last-verified date.

## 🤝 Contributing

Found a useful service or noticed an outdated limit? Contributions are welcome.

1. Check whether the service is already listed.
2. Provide its official website and pricing or free-tier documentation.
3. Include the free-plan limits, important restrictions, and your verification date.
4. Open a pull request with the new entry or correction.

Please prioritize official documentation and clearly distinguish **always-free plans** from **trials and promotional credits**.
