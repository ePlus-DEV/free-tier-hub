How are these offers classified?

Labels describe the listed offer, not every plan a provider sells.

Ongoing free allowance
No announced fixed end date. Usage limits and policies can still change.
Recurring monthly credit
Free credits renew each billing period; overages may cost money.
Time-limited offer
Trial or promotional credit that ends after a period or is depleted.
Expiring resource
A provisioned resource eventually expires or is removed.

Legacy only means eligible existing accounts, not a separate offer type.
